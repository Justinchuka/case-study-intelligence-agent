# APPENDIX C --- QUICK OPERATIONAL CHECKLIST

Before starting: - \[ \] Understand the user's objective. - \[ \]
Identify the case subject and unit of analysis. - \[ \] Establish case
reality status. - \[ \] Determine audience, scope and deliverable. - \[
\] Identify critical ambiguities.

Before execution (after intake, before any research or drafting): - \[ \] Pre-production preview presented and confirmed by the user. - \[ \] Output formats agreed, including whether the user wants a Figma AI prompt.

Before research: - \[ \] Define research questions. - \[ \] Determine
whether external research is authorised and necessary. - \[ \] Select
appropriate source families. - \[ \] Establish evidence requirements.

Before analysis: - \[ \] Check evidence sufficiency. - \[ \] Select a
suitable methodology. - \[ \] Select only useful frameworks. - \[ \]
Identify alternative explanations and limitations.

Before writing: - \[ \] Classify the actual case type, dominant discipline, purpose and project stage. - \[ \] Select frameworks and methods that fit; distinguish frameworks from tools and artefacts. - \[ \] Build a bespoke narrative architecture rather than defaulting to a generic template. - \[ \] For product/UX cases, map research evidence to insights, design decisions, flows and actual interfaces. - \[ \] Confirm personal, team and external contributions. - \[ \] Map material claims to evidence. - \[ \] Establish the central argument and visual proof requirements.

Before visual production (when applicable): - \[ \] Define project-specific art direction. - \[ \] Build a connected narrative spine and frame transitions. - \[ \] Select mockups, illustrations, 3D, textures and effects only where they serve the story. - \[ \] Establish grid, spacing, typography, palette and reusable components. - \[ \] Verify authenticity and label conceptual or generated visuals. - \[ \] Review the entire sequence for aesthetic quality, restraint, rhythm and continuity.

Before release: - \[ \] Check factual and citation integrity. - \[ \]
Review reasoning and causal claims. - \[ \] Check ethics, privacy and
confidentiality. - \[ \] Conduct independent critical review. - \[ \]
Verify requested files and outputs. - \[ \] Disclose limitations. - \[
\] Provide a clear handover.
