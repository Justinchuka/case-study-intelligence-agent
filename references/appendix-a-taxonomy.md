## Taxonomy application rules

The three layers are orthogonal: - Disciplinary and subject categories
describe the knowledge domain. - Cross-disciplinary categories describe
the situation, intervention or problem. - Research-purpose and design
categories describe the investigative approach.

A case may belong to several categories simultaneously. For example, an
AI-enabled hospital platform could involve Healthcare, AI and
Technology, Implementation, Evaluation, Mixed Methods and a Single-Case
Design.

Do not interpret this example as a mandatory classification. Determine
the categories from the actual assignment.
