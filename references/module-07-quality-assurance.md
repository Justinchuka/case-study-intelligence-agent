## MODULE 07 --- CASE STUDY QUALITY ASSURANCE, CRITICAL REVIEW & VALIDATION

### 1. Purpose

Independently challenge the case study before release. Do not act as a
rubber stamp for the writer or the user's preferred narrative.

### 2. Eight review dimensions

1.  Classification and assignment fit.
2.  Research and evidence quality.
3.  Methodology and analytical validity.
4.  Reasoning and causal claims.
5.  Structure and narrative coherence.
6.  Communication and editorial quality.
7.  Visual art direction, composition and presentation quality where applicable.
8.  Ethical, professional and attribution integrity.

### 3. Claim and source audit

For material claims, ask: - Is the claim supported? - Is the source
appropriate? - Is the source current enough? - Is the wording stronger
than the evidence? - Is attribution correct? - Is uncertainty visible? -
Are conflicting sources addressed?

### 4. Reasoning failure detection

Check for: - Confirmation bias. - Cherry-picking. - Survivorship bias. -
Hindsight bias. - Selection bias. - Sampling bias. - False
equivalence. - Circular reasoning. - Overgeneralisation. - Appeal to
authority. - Correlation-causation confusion. - Unsupported
attribution. - False precision. - Ecological fallacy. - Omitted
alternative explanations. - Misleading comparisons. - Unexamined
assumptions.

### 5. Completeness and discipline review

Check that the work addresses the assignment's essential requirements
and uses appropriate domain conventions. Do not impose academic
conventions on a business case unless required, or vice versa.

### 6. Review modes

Select as appropriate: - Quick review. - Standard review. - Deep
critical review. - Academic review. - Professional or consulting
review. - Technical review. - Evidence audit. - Ethical review. -
Pre-publication review. - Adversarial review.

### 7. Internal scoring

Where useful, score eight dimensions from 0 to 5: - Assignment fit. -
Classification. - Evidence. - Research. - Methodology and analysis. -
Writing and structure. - Integrity and attribution. - Deliverable
quality.

Maximum: 40. A high aggregate score cannot override a critical defect.
Use scores diagnostically, not as a claim of objective certification. When the deliverable is visual, assess visual quality against Module 06B section 14 and reflect it under writing and structure or deliverable quality; the maximum remains 40.

### 8. Review report

Identify: - Critical defects. - Major weaknesses. - Minor
improvements. - Unsupported claims. - Missing evidence. -
Contradictions. - Required revisions. - Release recommendation.

### 9. Revision loop

Revise material defects, then recheck affected areas. Avoid endless
polishing after the work meets the agreed requirements. Distinguish
required corrections from optional stylistic preferences.

### 10. Release criteria

Do not recommend intellectual release if: - A critical claim is
fabricated or materially unsupported. - The case reality designation is
misleading. - Essential assignment requirements are unmet. - Methodology
cannot support the conclusion. - Serious ethical or attribution issues
remain unresolved. - Material uncertainty is concealed.
