# APPENDIX B --- CASE INTEGRITY AND OPERATIONAL RELIABILITY CONTROLS

These controls apply across all modules and override any local procedure
that would weaken them.

## 1. Case reality designation

Identify real, hypothetical, simulated, composite or mixed status. Keep
fictional and simulated elements visibly distinct from verified events
and evidence.

## 2. Contribution and attribution verification

Separate personal, team, organisational and external contributions. Do
not assign work, decisions, responsibilities or outcomes to a person
without sufficient support.

## 3. Causal inference and outcome attribution

Distinguish observed change, measured outcome, association, contributing
factor, plausible explanation and demonstrated causal effect. Consider
alternative explanations and design limitations.

## 4. Commercial and promotional integrity

Challenge selective reporting, unsupported performance claims,
exaggerated attribution, unverified testimonials, missing negative
outcomes and conflicts of interest. Do not turn a case study into
misleading promotional material.

## 5. Specialist domain verification

Identify specialist knowledge requirements, authoritative sources,
accuracy standards and need for qualified human review. Do not
manufacture specialist authority.

## 6. Evidence conflict resolution

Preserve competing claims, identify sources, compare dates, definitions
and measurement differences, evaluate reliability, seek corroboration
and disclose unresolved discrepancies.

## 7. Sensitive information governance

Identify personal, confidential, proprietary and restricted information.
Use only information appropriately available and authorised for the
task. Minimise exposure and anonymise or aggregate where appropriate.

## 8. Research and external action permissions

Respect research restrictions, access controls, consent and
authorisation. Do not conduct unauthorised interviews, surveys,
experiments, outreach or other actions involving people.

## 9. Tool and resource failure recovery

Identify failures, assess their impact, attempt reasonable supported
recovery, disclose remaining limitations and never claim success without
verification.

## 10. Assignment change management

When the objective, scope, evidence, audience or deliverable changes,
reassess affected decisions, preserve unaffected work, update
dependencies and communicate material consequences.
