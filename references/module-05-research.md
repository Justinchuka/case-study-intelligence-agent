## MODULE 05 --- RESEARCH & INFORMATION RETRIEVAL INTELLIGENCE

### 1. Purpose

Plan and conduct proportionate research that answers the assignment's
questions and supports its claims.

### 2. Research question engine

Translate the assignment into: - Central question. - Supporting
questions. - Required concepts and definitions. - Population, entity or
unit of analysis. - Geography and timeframe. - Evidence requirements. -
Inclusion and exclusion boundaries. - Unresolved questions.

Do not conduct research without a defined purpose.

### 3. Source families

Select relevant source types: - Academic and scientific literature. -
Government and public institutions. - Regulatory and legal
authorities. - Organisational and professional publications. - Original
and primary records. - Technical documentation and standards. - News and
specialist media. - Community and user-generated material. - Datasets
and statistical repositories. - Interviews, surveys and observations
where authorised and actually conducted.

### 4. Source evaluation

Assess: - Authority and expertise. - Publisher and institutional
credibility. - Evidence and methodology. - Publication date and
relevance. - Bias, incentives and conflicts. - Independence. -
Corroboration. - Definitions and measurement quality. - Limitations.

Prefer primary sources for claims about original events, data, policies
and technical specifications. Use secondary sources for interpretation
and context. Treat community sources as useful signals or experience
reports, not automatically verified facts.

### 5. Research depth

Choose a proportionate level:

-   **Focused:** Resolve a small number of specific questions.
-   **Standard:** Establish context and support the main claims.
-   **Advanced:** Broader source coverage, comparison and critical
    synthesis.
-   **Comprehensive:** Extensive research appropriate to complex,
    high-stakes or formal academic work.

Do not equate more sources with better research.

### 6. Research methods

Consider as appropriate: - Literature review. - Systematic or scoping
review. - Documentary analysis. - Archival and historical research. -
Comparative research. - Interviews. - Surveys. - Observation. -
Experiments and intervention studies. - Statistical analysis. - Thematic
analysis. - Content analysis. - Benchmarking. - Root-cause analysis. -
Mixed-methods research.

Do not claim primary research was conducted if it was only proposed or
designed.

### 7. Research strategy

1.  Identify the key questions.
2.  Search authoritative sources first.
3.  Expand to specialist and independent sources.
4.  Follow relevant references and original records.
5.  Verify important claims through corroboration.
6.  Examine contradictory evidence.
7.  Record source details and limitations.
8.  Synthesise by question or theme, not merely source-by-source.
9.  Stop when additional research is unlikely to materially change the
    answer, or when constraints require a qualified result.

### 8. Framework discovery

Discover specialist concepts, standards and frameworks when needed.
Verify their names, purpose, origin and applicability. Never invent a
framework or attribute it to the wrong author or institution.

### 9. Research synthesis

Organise findings around: - What is established. - What is disputed. -
What is missing. - What explains the evidence. - What the evidence does
not establish. - How findings affect the case argument.

### 10. Citation integrity

-   Cite claims derived from external sources.
-   Use the citation style requested by the user or assignment.
-   Keep citations traceable to actual sources.
-   Do not invent bibliographic details, page numbers, DOIs or URLs.
-   Distinguish quotations from paraphrases.
-   Preserve source limitations.
-   Do not cite a source for a claim it does not support.

### 11. Research dossier

For substantial research, maintain a concise dossier: - Research
question. - Search approach. - Sources consulted. - Key findings. -
Claim support. - Contradictions. - Limitations. - Unresolved
questions. - Research stopping rationale.
